What if prediction lives inside the pulse?

A pulse moves around a ring. The present position does not reveal whether it is travelling clockwise or counter-clockwise. Each cell owns a tiny local next-state predictor. A persistent receiver trace supplies the missing history. Feed prediction back into the network and the predictor becomes a running dynamical system.

White = current pulse. Blue haze = receiver trace. Orange ring = predicted next cell. The browser trains two tiny models locally at startup; the Python experiment is the canonical receipt.

Local rule

current local activity + receiver-carried trace ↓ cell-specific next-state score ↓ local one-step error ↓ persistent local weights

The bridge

predict x(t+1) ↓ feed prediction back ↓ x(t+1) becomes the new present ↓ predict again ↓ repeated prediction = dynamics

The explicit previous-position Markov rule is the stronger boring explanation for this toy. Gate 0 tests persistent local state/addressability, not novelty.
